Our Story

From a tiny home kitchen to your neighborhood favourite.

Sugar Rush began with a simple dream — to share the warmth of freshly baked goods made from time-honoured recipes. Every loaf, pastry, and cake is crafted by hand using the finest natural ingredients.

We believe baking is an act of love. That's why we wake before sunrise, source locally, and never compromise on quality. Whether it's a flaky croissant or a celebration cake, we pour our hearts into every creation.
